KPI & Metric Dictionary

What every number on the Overview tab means and exactly how it's calculated.

Data population & methodology

  • "Record Type Indonesia" and "Record Type 12" are the same filter — Indonesia's record type ID in this CRM is 12.
  • Population: every OPEN Indonesia opportunity regardless of age (so no old stale lead is missed), plus any opportunity — open or closed — created in the last 12 months. Indonesia has 72,721 Opportunity records all‑time; fetching every one blew both the platform's request timeout and its memory limit, so closed/won history beyond 12 months isn't included. This population is what "Total" / "opportunities matched" refers to everywhere below unless a metric says otherwise.
  • Data is refreshed by a background job roughly every 15 minutes (a full pull is ~73k records — too slow to run per page load), not sampled and not read off the CRM's own filter UI — that UI's per‑field filters proved unreliable for the Service Level field.
  • "Last Mile – Parcel" and "Last Mile – Document" are excluded except for Raden Roro Inggil Pratiwi's opportunities (a data‑quality rule, not a CRM limitation).
  • Stage includes Prospecting — a legacy value no longer offered in the CRM's own stage filter dropdown.
  • Any record with "UNAUTHORIZED OPPORTUNITY" in its name is excluded from every count as a non‑pipeline test artifact.
  • Open (used throughout as "open deals" / "open + future") means every stage except Closed–Won and Closed–Lost — this includes Future Opportunity.

Top summary cards

  • Created This Month / vs last month: count of opportunities (any stage) whose created_at falls in the current / previous calendar month.
  • Open Pipeline: count of all OPEN deals (open_active + Future Opportunity).
  • CRM Updated %: the final combined Activity KPI — see "Activity" below for the full definition.
  • Closed–Won / Closed–Lost (This Month) / vs last month: count of deals whose stage is Closed–Won / Closed–Lost and whose stage_last_changed_at (falling back to updated_at) falls in the current / previous month — i.e. deals that actually closed this month, not deals merely created this month.
  • Win Rate: closed_won ÷ (closed_won + closed_lost) over the full population (12mo + all‑time open). Answers "of deals that reached a final outcome, how many did we win?" — still‑open deals are excluded from both sides of the ratio.
  • Pipeline Conversion: closed_won ÷ total — total includes still‑open deals, so this answers "of every opportunity ever in this population, what share ended up won?" (necessarily lower than Win Rate, since it also counts deals that haven't concluded yet).
  • Potential Rev / Mth: sum of total_potential_revenue_mth (and, for the sub‑label, committed_revenue_mth) across the full population.

Deals by Stage & Pipeline Composition

  • Pipeline Composition (donut): the full population split into four outcome buckets — Active pipeline, Future opportunity, Closed–Won, Closed–Lost — as a share of the total.
  • Deals by Stage (bars below the donut): count of deals currently sitting in each stage, ordered by the sales process sequence (Active pipeline), then Closed & parked (Closed–Won, Closed–Lost, Future Opportunity).
  • The percentage next to each section header (Active pipeline, Closed & parked) is that section's share of the total — it matches the corresponding donut bucket(s) above (Closed & parked = Future opportunity + Closed–Won + Closed–Lost combined).

Product Line, Service Level, Top Salesperson & Ageing

  • All four consider OPEN deals only (open + Future Opportunity) — closed–won/lost are excluded, unlike Deals by Stage above.
  • Deals by NV Product Line: count of open deals per nv_product_line value.
  • Deals by Service Level: service_level is a multi‑select field, so one deal can add to more than one bar — percentages are share‑of‑open‑pipeline per tag, not a partition. "Blank" counts deals with no Service Level tag at all.
  • Top Salesperson: open deal count per owner_name, ranked descending. Each rep's bar is itself split into ageing segments — for every OPEN deal, days = today − created_at (purely creation age, no Notebook fallback), bucketed into 0‑7d, 7‑15d, 15‑30d, 30d+ (see the color legend above the chart) — so a rep with a long bar mostly in the 30d+ color has a lot of ageing pipeline, not just a lot of pipeline.

Activity

  • Two independent freshness signals feed one final KPI: Notebook (a note logged on the Opportunity) and Tasks (CRM Task records) — a rep can be current on one and stale on the other.
  • Notebook: entries are fetched separately from Opportunities (GET /notebook/entries?object_type=Opportunity, paginated) and reduced to the most recent entry's created_at per opportunity id. For every OPEN deal, last_touch = that Notebook date, or created_at if the deal has no Notebook entry at all (never touched ≠ recently touched); days = today − last_touch, bucketed into 4 tiers: < 7d (green), 7‑14d (yellow), 14‑30d (orange), 30d+ (red / not touched). "Notebook touched in last 7 days" = count in the <7d tier ÷ total open deals.
  • Tasks: population is CRM Task records (not Opportunities) with status in Not Started or In Progress — Completed and Deferred are excluded entirely. Task is a global object with no country field, so it's fetched pre‑filtered server‑side to just the owner_ids in the Indonesia team roster (a rep only owns their own country's work), not a full global pull. A task counts as Active when both hold: (1) last_touch (=updated_at, falling back to created_at if never edited since logged) is within the last 7 days, AND (2) it is not overdue (due_date is blank, or today or later). "Tasks active in last 7 days" = Active task count ÷ total open task count.
  • CRM Updated % (the final KPI, also shown as a top summary card): Notebook and Task counts are summed, not averaged — (Notebook <7d count + Task Active count) ÷ (total open deals + total open Tasks). Summing means a rep with many open deals but few Tasks isn't equally weighted between the two signals; it's one pooled ratio, not an average of two percentages.
  • By salesperson table: one row per rep. CRM Updated (%) is that rep's own version of the final KPI (their Notebook <7d + Task Active, over their own open deals + open Tasks), followed by the Notebook detail (Updated % plus its 7‑14d/14‑30d/30d+ counts and Total — the <7d count is omitted since the % column already shows it) and the Task detail (Updated %, Updated Task count, Open Tasks). Sorted worst‑first on CRM Updated (%); a rep with no open deals but open Tasks (or vice versa) still gets a row, with "–" on the side that doesn't apply. Reps with no owner_id on file in the team roster can't be queried for Tasks at all — their Task columns show "–" and their CRM Updated (%) falls back to Notebook‑only (not directly comparable to reps with both signals); they're named in the caption below the table.

Stage Bottlenecks & Stage × Ageing

  • Both use stage_last_changed_at (falling back to created_at) — i.e. how long a deal has sat in its current stage, not since it was created.
  • Stage Bottlenecks: average days‑in‑current‑stage per stage, across OPEN deals only, longest first.
  • Stage × Ageing: for each stage, the same day buckets used throughout (0‑7d/7‑15d/15‑30d/30d+), shown as a composition bar — Share mode normalizes every stage's bar to 100%; Count mode scales bar width by the stage's total deal count so stages can be compared by volume too.
  • Avg lead time to Closed–Won (below the Stage Bottlenecks table): for every Closed–Won deal, days = stage_last_changed_at (or updated_at) − created_at. The CRM has no separate "entered New stage" timestamp, so created_at is used as a proxy for it — the same approximation the ageing buckets rely on.

Pipeline Strength (Forecast)

  • Population: OPEN deals with an expected_close_date (deals with none are excluded and counted in the panel's caption).
  • Overdue: expected_close_date < today. Everything else is bucketed by the month of expected_close_date, starting at the current month and continuing through however far into the future the data goes (no fixed 3‑month cap).
  • Each bar is stacked by stage and labeled with count + potential revenue, so it's visible whether deals due to close soon are actually in a late stage (Agreed to Ship / Ready to Ship) or an unrealistically early one.
  • Bar height uses a square‑root scale (not linear) so low‑count months next to a high‑count one don't flatten to an invisible sliver — the printed count above each bar is always the real number.

Created Opportunities by Manager & Salesperson

  • Population: the full dataset (open + closed), grouped by the month of created_at, for the current month and the two before it.
  • By Manager: each opportunity's owner_name is looked up in a curated team roster (Active reps only — see backend/team_roster.json) to find that rep's Manager; owners not found in the roster are grouped as "Unmapped" (named in the panel's caption).
  • By Salesperson: one row per Active rep in the roster, including reps who created nothing in this window (shown as 0) — unlike the other tables on this dashboard, this one isn't built only from matched deals, so a quiet rep is still visible.
  • Target (current month column only): each rep is expected to create 10 opportunities a month; a manager's target is 10 × their headcount (reps reporting to them, per the roster). The bar fills to min(count ÷ target, 100%) and is colored red (<50%), orange (50‑74%), yellow (75‑99%), or green (≥100%). Earlier months show a plain count — the target is only meaningful for the month still in progress.
